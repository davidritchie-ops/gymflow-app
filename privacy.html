Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Overview

GymFlow ("the App") is a workout tracking application for iPhone and Apple Watch developed by David Ritchie. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

The App collects and stores the following data that you provide:

  • Workout data: Exercise names, sets, reps, weights, RIR (reps in reserve), and session timestamps.
  • Training program data: Workout phase names, exercise templates, and weekly targets imported from Google Sheets.
  • Google account email: Used solely for authenticating with Google Sheets to import and sync your training data.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: All workout data is stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • iCloud (CloudKit): If you have iCloud enabled, your workout data syncs between your iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is not accessible to us.
  • Google Sheets: If you choose to connect your Google account, completed workout sessions are synced to your personal Google Sheets spreadsheet. This data is stored in your own Google account.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use any third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not store your Google password. Authentication uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain.
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following services at your discretion:

  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): For syncing data between your devices. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sheets API: For importing training programs and syncing completed sessions. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • You can delete all local data by deleting the App from your devices.
  • You can revoke Google Sheets access at any time through your Google Account settings.
  • iCloud data can be managed through your device's iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
